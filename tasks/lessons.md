# Lessons

[2026-11-05] | I scored the game as a Lobby-to-F4 slice and planned to end it after F4, so F3 to F1 had no round. The owner says those floors are supposed to exist. | Score and plan against the full five-floor game. Never cut planned content from the roadmap without asking.
[2026-11-06] | My R34 plan had Marty chase and down players. The owner wants a psychological arc: trust, then misleading, no catch or chase yet. | Read Story and Lore and ask about tone before planning a threat. Never assume a monster means chases.
[2026-11-09] | I recommended Marty v3 from Downloads over the owner-picked M1, and it was the wrong model. | When the owner names a model or file, use that exact file. Do not swap in a newer revision found elsewhere without asking.
[2026-11-13] | codex exec in a read-only sandbox on Windows could not start a shell (CreateProcessWithLogonW 267) and read no files | Pipe source into codex exec on stdin with line numbers and tell it not to run tools
[2026-11-17] | Floor 3 shipped with 11 of 13 jobs as the same hold-E, dialogue built on the 'X, so Y' shape, and a bypass whose story purpose (raw water to the town) the game never stated. The owner called it repetitive, disconnected and AI-sounding. | Give each job its own verb or decision. Have every floor state how it moves Marty's plan to contaminate the town's water.
